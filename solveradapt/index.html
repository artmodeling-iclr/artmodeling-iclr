Purpose

Turn USD joint drives and physics attributes into editable controls, side-view motion previews, and reviewed saves.

Editable properties

Drive targets, stiffness, damping, maximum force, MassAPI, MaterialAPI, scene gravity, joint axes, and limits.

Preview scope

The browser shows a lightweight physics preview. Verify final behavior in Isaac Sim or PhysX.
